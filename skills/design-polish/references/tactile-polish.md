# Tactile Design Engineering Details

1. **Hit Targets**: Minimum 44x44px clickable area on mobile, even for small icons.
2. **Active States**: Subtle compression `active:scale-[0.98]` gives buttons a physical feel.
3. **Border Hierarchy**: Use subtle semi-transparent borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` on dark mode) instead of heavy solid lines.
4. **Text Balance**: Apply `text-wrap: balance` on headlines to eliminate orphan words.
