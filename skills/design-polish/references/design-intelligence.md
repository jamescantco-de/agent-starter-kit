# UI/UX Style & Intelligence Guidelines

1. **Design Archetypes**:
   - **Modern Technical**: Clean neutral slate, monospace accents, razor borders, subtle dot grids.
   - **Accessible Luxury**: Deep rich tones, refined serif/sans pairing, generous negative space.
   - **Playful Tactile**: Soft rounded cards, bold pill badges, warm saturated accents, tactile shadows.

2. **Colour Hierarchy (60-30-10 Rule)**:
   - 60% Dominant canvas/background (e.g. Midnight Slate #101420 or Ghost White).
   - 30% Structural cards, borders, and secondary surfaces.
   - 10% High-contrast accent reserved strictly for CTAs and active states.
