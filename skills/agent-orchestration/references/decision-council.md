# 4-Voice Decision Council Protocol

When faced with an ambiguous architectural or commercial choice:
1. **The Architect**: Argues for long-term purity, decoupling, and structural elegance.
2. **The Pragmatist**: Argues for minimum code, immediate shipping speed, and simple boring tech.
3. **The Adversary**: Attacks fragile assumptions, edge-case failure modes, and security risks.
4. **The Operator**: Focuses on maintainability, cognitive overhead, and day-to-day developer experience.
