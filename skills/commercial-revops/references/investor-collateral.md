# Investor Update Memo Template

1. **Executive Snapshot**: 2-sentence summary of primary milestone achieved this month.
2. **Key Metrics**: MRR / ARR, MoM Growth, Net Revenue Retention, Cash Balance & Runway.
3. **Highlights**: Product releases shipped, marquee customer wins, strategic hires.
4. **Lowlights & Blockers**: Missed goals and candid diagnostic explanations.
5. **The Asks**: Specific intros, talent referrals, or customer connections needed.
