# Anti-Cliché Voice Profile & British English Rules

1. **Banned Words**: Never use: delve, seamless, transformative, cutting-edge, synergy, tapestry, myriad, plethora, embark, foster, holistic, paradigm, elevate, harness, unlock, actionable, move the needle.
2. **British English**: Always write organisation, recognise, whilst, programme, learnt, specialised, colour, behaviour.
3. **Tone**: Humble, direct, practical, grounded in real numbers and authentic builder experience.
