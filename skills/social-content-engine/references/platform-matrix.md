# Multi-Platform Formatting Constraints

1. **X (Twitter)**: Max 280 chars per tweet (or Long-Form Article for Premium). Thread opening hook must establish immediate tension/outcome.
2. **Bluesky**: Strictly max 300 UTF-8 characters per post. Rich link facets must be explicitly declared with byte ranges.
3. **Cross-Posting Rule**: Never post identical copy across platforms. Adapt spacing, vocabulary, and calls-to-action per channel.
